**Recommended direction: an editorial workflow film, 16 seconds, silent.** Make the *scope* the visual anchor: an ivory task sheet moves between buyer and supplier, a clay $180 revision is visibly rejected against an approved **$120 ceiling**, and a **$120 receipt** arrives with **human acceptance pending**. Treat it as an explicitly labeled *illustrative research scenario*, not footage of a live product or evidence of a completed transaction.

## Exactly three creative territories

| Territory | Visual grammar | How the silent story reads | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. The Scope Folio** | Ivory paper, limestone shadow, sage ruling, clay correction marks; Newsreader headlines and Public Sans annotations. Inspired by the *editorial principles* in BUCK’s Firebrand and Asana work. [web:94][web:3] | Pages reveal the approved task, a rejected revision, then a receipt awaiting a person’s decision. | Most distinctive and tactile; paper handling alone may obscure who made each decision. |
| **2. The Accord Path — recommended** | A restrained editorial sheet **plus** a diagram: two role columns, one approved scope, one boundary, one pending endpoint. Takes the clarity of Modern Treasury’s physical-system idea without depicting money movement. [web:76] | The viewer follows one task through approved scope → rejected out-of-scope proposal → receipt → pending human acceptance. | Best balance of brand character and comprehension; demands meticulous label hierarchy. |
| **3. The Material Span** | A sculptural limestone/brass A or bridge, with precise 2D labels appearing alongside it; informed by BUCK’s material system and ManvsMachine’s coherent CGI form. [web:1][web:55] | A boundary forms, stops an over-ceiling branch, then frames the pending receipt. | Strongest brand-signature potential, but the weakest choice if 3D beauty displaces the actual task states. |

### Recommended 16-second cut: The Accord Path

**Persistent small label:** “Illustrative research scenario.” These are proposed timings for TaskAccord—not reported timings of any reference film.

| Time | Shot and on-screen copy | Motion and transition | Framing priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:02** | Newsreader: **“One task. One approved scope.”** Accord Span draws a short sage rule. | Begin on a composed still; the rule reveals a single paper task sheet. | The statement must read immediately, even with sound off. |
| **0:02–0:04.5** | Sheet between **Buyer** and **Supplier**: “Approved scope” · **“Ceiling $120.”** | A brass boundary settles around the sheet; no coins, checkout or payment UI. | Make the ceiling the clearest numeric label. |
| **0:04.5–0:07.5** | A second slip enters: **“Revision $180”** → **“Rejected — above approved ceiling.”** | Slip approaches the boundary, stops, receives a clay rejection mark, and exits. Hold the rejected state long enough to read. | Keep **$180** and **Rejected** together; never make the revision appear approved or performed. |
| **0:07.5–0:10.5** | Return to the original approved scope and **$120 ceiling**. | Match the departing slip’s edge to the original sheet’s edge; the approved path remains the only continuous line. | Re-establish that the rejected proposal did not change the scope. |
| **0:10.5–0:13.5** | **“Receipt $120”** appears at the end of the path. | A receipt sheet arrives and stops. No “paid,” “settled,” “verified” or acceptance checkmark. | Distinguish receipt amount from ceiling by label, not color alone. |
| **0:13.5–0:16** | **“Human acceptance pending”** beside the receipt; resolve to TaskAccord Span/A. | Hold the pending state, then make a small, calm mark reveal. **End rather than auto-looping the narrative.** | Leave both the pending status and brand legible in the final frame. |

**Pacing rule:** one meaningful state change per shot, with the rejection and pending acceptance getting the longest readable holds. The edit should feel like following a document through a bounded decision, not watching a product demo.

| Motion-ready still-image need | What to prepare |
|---|---|
| **Complete storyboard states** | Six approved stills corresponding to the shots above, including a fully readable rejection state and final pending state—not just attractive transition frames. |
| **Layered editorial kit** | Separate paper faces/edges, fibers, shadows, sage rules, clay rejection mark, brass boundary, buyer/supplier labels, receipt and logo; establish type sizes and contrast before animation. |
| **Brand-material study** | One optional limestone/brass Span/A render with lighting and texture passes. Use it at the close, not behind small financial labels. |
| **Responsive and static masters** | A wide composition, a purpose-reframed narrow composition, and one standalone poster containing **approved scope, $120 ceiling, rejected $180 revision, $120 receipt, and human acceptance pending**. |

**Placement and controls.** On desktop, give the diagram a broad central field with generous side margins; keep the hero headline and CTA *below* the film, not obscured by it. On mobile, recompose into a vertical sequence rather than cropping the desktop diagram: stack Buyer, scope and Supplier, and keep each amount adjacent to its status. Provide a visible **Pause/Play** control, play once by default, and show the complete static poster when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is set. W3C guidance calls for a pause, stop or hide mechanism for qualifying automatically moving content lasting over five seconds; the reduced-motion media feature conveys a user preference to minimize nonessential motion.
