01 / Research before drawing

The mark should carry the idea, at sixteen pixels.

TaskAccord records a bounded task between human legal entities. The strongest visual territory is a crossing with a boundary and a traceable handoff. Map relief, limestone and brass express that idea at large scale; the logo must retain its silhouette in a single color.

Public-web research did not establish an operating exact-name TaskAccord product. It found adjacent uses of “Accord,” including Ontra's contract product and Accord Contracts. That check is inconclusive, not name or trademark clearance.

Authentic material cues

Texas survey archives inform contour hierarchy, measured rules, parcel divisions and annotations. Early printing informs proportion and letterpress restraint. All supplied imagery and diagrams are original; no archive map has been traced or reproduced.

Lockup and reproduction

  • Symbol master: 64 × 64 units. Clearspace: 16 units, or one-quarter of symbol height.
  • Wordmark gap: 18 units in the horizontal lockup. Preserve supplied path outlines and kerning.
  • Minimum horizontal logo: 156px on screen / 32mm in print. Minimum standalone mark: 24px.
  • Favicon exception: supplied raster artwork at 16 / 32 / 48px; do not substitute a tiny wordmark.
  • Ink on ivory is primary. Paper on ink is the reverse. Black is the production monochrome version.
  • No shadows, gradients, distortion, extra rings, distress effects, animated letterforms or brass textures inside the logo.

07 / Image, material and shape library

One landscape.
Many useful views.

Original image assets supplied: homepage survey crossing poster, secondary-page mesa passage poster, and clean ivory paper texture. PNG masters and optimized WebP derivatives are included. These are visual assets and still fallbacks, not operating 3D scenes.

Shape vocabulary: contour strata, paper planes, two-party spans, arch frames, narrow boundary shutters, receipt stacks and straight registration rules. Keep the same brass roughness, limestone scale, warm light direction and neutral paper across pages.

Homepage / Surveyor's Crossing · generated original environment, static poster.

Other public pages / Mesa Passage · generated original environment, static poster.

Material restraint

Use paper grain at 6–10% overlay opacity on reading surfaces, at a natural 512–768px scale. Never texture the glyphs or controls. Flat forms and legal text get clean ivory surfaces. Do not assert this texture is a perfectly seamless tile until its edges have been visually checked.

Composition rules

3D objects leave at least 40% low-detail negative space near headlines. Use 8:5 or 3:2 hero posters; 5:3 module illustrations; 4:3 explainers; a purpose-composed portrait camera for mobile. Do not stretch or simply squeeze the desktop landscape.

Photography, if later used, must be owned/licensed and context-relevant. No borrowed company offices, fake team portraits, flags, official seals or historic-document reproductions.

10 / Continuous 3D journey and motion specification

Scroll through
the same task.

The homepage uses one persistent environment and one camera, tied to native scroll progress. Module panels and copy are readable HTML over and beside the canvas. Separate public pages use closer Mesa Passage views and local task mechanisms. These camera scenes are specified here; live WebGL has not been built.

0–15% / Arrival

The survey

Oblique view over cream contour terraces and the copper crossing. Buyer and supplier sit at opposite endpoints. Hero explains the audience and research-stage status.

15–30% / Authority

The principals

Camera descends to the first pier. Recorded human owner and terms reference resolve alongside the task artifact. Human approval remains explicit.

30–45% / Scope

The approved path

Camera tracks the same paper record along the span. Deliverable, exclusions and acceptance criteria appear as controlled layers.

45–60% / Exception

The boundary

Short lateral camera move reveals a side branch. The $180 revision stops; $120 ceiling stays unchanged. No celebratory savings counter.

60–80% / Evidence

The receipt landing

Camera moves toward the receiving terrace. Same ID and receipt reference remain in view. Acceptance is pending; the assigned human next action appears.

80–100% / Discussion

The working table

Camera settles into a quiet paper-and-stone plane. Five offers, price hypotheses, limitations and joint application are readable without further movement.

Performance and access

Pause/skip controls, still-view option, keyboard/touch access and complete reduced-motion fallback. Critical copy and CTAs remain available if WebGL fails.

Initial budgets to test: ≤1.5MB hero poster, ≤4MB first 3D geometry/textures, compressed assets, at most 1.5 device-pixel-ratio on heavy scenes, pause rendering when hidden. Measure actual low-end mobile results before increasing complexity.

Use only one ambient canvas; do not add five independent WebGL scenes to cards. Prefer local vector animations for diagrams. No automatic sound or intro that blocks the site.
